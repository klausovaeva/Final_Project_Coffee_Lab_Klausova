A drink for everyone

Explore the characteristics and compositions of classic espresso-based drinks.

Ristretto

A short, intense shot using half the water of a standard espresso.

Espresso

The pure heart of coffee: a balanced 30ml shot brewed under pressure.

Doppio

A double espresso shot, providing twice the volume and intensity.

Lungo

A "long" shot where more water is pulled through the grounds.

Americano

Espresso diluted with hot water for a profile similar to drip coffee.

Macchiato

Espresso "stained" with a small dollop of frothed milk.

Cortado

Equal parts espresso and warm milk to balance the acidity.

Cappuccino

A classic third-based mix of espresso, steamed milk, and foam.

Caffe Latte

A milk-heavy drink with espresso and a thin layer of microfoam.

Flat White

Similar to a latte but with a higher coffee-to-milk ratio.

Affogato

A shot of espresso poured over a scoop of vanilla gelato.

Caffe Mocha

A chocolate-flavored variant of a latte, perfect for dessert.
