Global bean varieties & profiles

Understanding the origin of your beans is the first step in mastering the flavor profile of your cup. Use the data below to compare regional characteristics.

Coffee origin & flavor profile matrix
Origin country Typical variety Flavor notes Acidity level Ideal roast
Ethiopia Heirloom Blueberry, Jasmine, Bergamot High Light
Colombia Caturra Caramel, Red Apple, Nutty Medium Medium
Brazil Bourbon Chocolate, Peanut, Low Acid Low Medium-Dark
Kenya SL-28 Blackcurrant, Tomato, Grapefruit Very High Light
Sumatra Typica Earthy, Tobacco, Cedar Low Dark
Source: International Coffee Organization (ICO) Regional Data 2026.

Understanding terroir

Just like wine, coffee is a product of its environment. Altitude, soil composition, and processing methods all contribute to the final sensory experience described in the table above. Find your beans here: https://www.kofio.cz/kava.
